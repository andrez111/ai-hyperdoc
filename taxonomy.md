# Macro Taxonomy

Use exactly one approved macro.

## Approved macros

### AI

Use for artificial intelligence, generative AI, LLMs, agents, Copilot, prompting, AI adoption, and AI transformation.

### Business

Use for business ideas, startups, business models, product-market validation, entrepreneurship, commercialization, pricing, and market-entry strategy.

### Career

Use for career strategy, professional positioning, CV, LinkedIn, compensation, skills, and role progression.

### Finance

Use for banking, corporate finance, financial analysis, economics, accounting, financial regulation, and financial news when portfolio allocation is not the primary objective.

### Images

Use when the main objective is to create, edit, transform, or design a visual asset, regardless of the subject depicted.

### Investments

Use for portfolios, ETFs, equities, bonds, crypto, asset allocation, hedging, investment strategy, and buy/sell/hold decisions.

### Jobs

Use for job searches, vacancies, target employers, applications, hiring opportunities, and employer lists.

### Legal

Use for laws, contracts, legal analysis, compliance, and legal interpretation when no more specific macro dominates.

### Politics

Use for elections, political actors, public policy, government, political accountability, civic institutions, and geopolitics centered on political decisions or actors.

### RealEstate

Use for property acquisition, housing, property valuation, buildings, property regulation, and real-estate portfolios when the property itself is the primary subject.

### Research

Use for structured investigations, source discovery, evidence gathering, or comparative research when no clear domain-specific macro better represents the subject.

### Technology

Use for software, hardware, IT systems, architecture, cybersecurity, digital platforms, and enterprise technology when AI is not the central subject.

### Travel

Use for destinations, itineraries, transportation, accommodation, and trip planning.

### Other

Use only when no approved macro fits with reasonable confidence.

## Boundary rules

### Investments vs Finance

Use `Investments` when the user is deciding how to allocate, buy, sell, hold, hedge, or evaluate capital.

Use `Finance` for financial systems, banking, accounting, corporate finance, economics, or analysis not centered on a portfolio decision.

### Jobs vs Career

Use `Jobs` for external opportunities, vacancies, target employers, applications, and hiring searches.

Use `Career` for professional positioning, CV/LinkedIn, skills, compensation, role evolution, and long-term career strategy.

### AI vs Technology

Use `AI` when AI, LLMs, agents, Copilot, prompting, or AI adoption are central.

Use `Technology` for broader IT, software, infrastructure, cybersecurity, architecture, and digital tools when AI is not central.

### Business vs domain categories

Use `Business` when the main task is business-model design, validation, commercialization, pricing, market entry, or entrepreneurship.

Use the domain category when the conversation is primarily about the domain itself.

Example: a political accountability platform may be:
- `Politics` when discussing political data and accountability logic;
- `Technology` when discussing software architecture;
- `Business` when discussing monetization or market validation.

### Research vs domain categories

Prefer the domain category whenever the investigation clearly belongs to one domain.

Use `Research` when the research process itself is central or the subject is genuinely cross-domain.

### RealEstate vs Investments

Use `RealEstate` when properties, buildings, locations, valuations, housing rules, or real-estate portfolios are the primary object.

Use `Investments` when real estate is only one asset class in a broader allocation discussion.

### Images vs depicted subject

Use `Images` when producing or editing the visual is the primary objective.

Do not classify an image-generation request as `Politics`, `Travel`, or another depicted subject merely because that subject appears in the image.
